About Maryam

PhD Researcher. Community Builder. Research Communication Advocate.

I am a doctoral researcher at Kingston University, where my research sits at the intersection of leadership, decision-making and emerging technology contexts.

Alongside my PhD, I work as a Research Associate on the 6G-VERSUS project, a Horizon Europe funded initiative exploring the societal and environmental impact of emerging 6G infrastructure.

I am also the founder of InsightSpace.Co, a community helping PhD researchers communicate their ideas beyond academia. Through content, workshops and community building, I work to make research more accessible, memorable and impactful for the people it is meant to serve.

Flagship Community Initiative

InsightSpace.Co

How do we take complex academic knowledge and make it usable, memorable, and actionable beyond academia?

InsightSpace.Co is a digital community for PhD researchers, post graduate academics and early career academics. Through content, live sessions and workshops, we help researchers communicate their work clearly and compelling, making research more accessible and impactful beyond the institution.
