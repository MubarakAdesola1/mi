About Maryam

Bridging the gap between the boardroom, the lab, and the product.

I connect UX research, behavioural science, and doctoral scholarship through one core inquiry: how do people make decisions in complex and uncertain environments?

In my PhD research at Kingston University, I examine how organizational leaders navigate sustainability contexts where cognitive biases, heuristic traps, and shifting regulations can cloud risk perception. I focus on creating governance systems that scaffold sound, ethical, and forward-looking decisions.

In my UX research practice, I apply that same analytical rigor to product, service, and organizational design. Using qualitative inquiry, deep interviews, and behavioural data coding, I uncover how stakeholders actually behave — not just what they say — and translate those findings into actionable strategies.
